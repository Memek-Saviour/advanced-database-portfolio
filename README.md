## Selected Case Study: SaviourPay Digital Bank (Simulated)

**Scenario:** SaviourPay is a simulated Nigerian digital bank and wallet platform offering mobile-first banking to individuals and small businesses.

**Nature of the system:** A transactional banking platform handling customer accounts, wallets, transfers, bill payments and fraud monitoring.

**Users:** Customers, bank operations staff, customer support agents, compliance/audit officers, fraud analysts.

**Major data stored:** Customer profiles and KYC details, accounts and wallet balances, transactions (deposits, withdrawals, transfers, reversals), beneficiaries, branches and partner banks, audit logs, fraud alerts.

**Key operations:** Open an account, deposit and withdraw, transfer funds, reverse failed transactions, retrieve statements, audit balances, flag suspicious transactions.
